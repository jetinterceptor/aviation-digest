10. FAA and EASA Harmonize Airworthiness Directives on Fuel and Fluid Shutoff Valves

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) and the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) have issued coordinated final-rule Airworthiness Directives addressing potential internal leakage in critical landing gear selector valves (LGSVs) and motorized fuel shutoff actuators. Citing publications in the Federal Register (GovInfo) and EASA Safety Publications, the harmonized regulations mandate repetitive functional leak checks, installation limits on designated batch serial numbers, and mandatory replacement with upgraded, corrosion-resistant valve assemblies. The agencies provided detailed compliance templates to streamline maintenance record-keeping for Part 121 airlines, corporate operators, and approved Part 145 repair stations.
